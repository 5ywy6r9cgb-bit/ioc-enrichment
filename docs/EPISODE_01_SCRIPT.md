## PRODUCTION RULES — these are not suggestions

1. **Every factual claim gets a citation card on screen** — docket number,
   award ID, or FEC schedule. If it has no card, it does not get said.
2. **No B-roll of a data centre under a sentence about bots.** That is the
   exact edit this episode exists to argue against.
3. **No person's face under an allegation they are not charged with.**
4. **Every dollar figure gets its qualifier spoken**, not just supered:
   "a floor", "obligated-plus-potential", "face value".
5. **Where the record stops, the narration stops.** The phrase is
   *"and that is where the record ends"* — use it. It is the whole brand.

## SEGMENT 4 — SO WHERE ARE THE BOT FARMS? — 6:15–8:15

**NARRATION**

> Now the question I get asked most. **Are the data centres bot farms?**
>
> **No.** And I want to be careful here, because this is where people like me
> lose credibility, and once you lose it you do not get it back.
>
> "Bot farm" is four different things wearing one name.

> **[GRAPHIC — four rows, built one at a time]**
> **Click-fraud rings** → rented cloud capacity → *rented, never owned*
> **SIM farms** → warehouses of physical phones → *not a data centre*
> **Persona operations** → ordinary servers and proxies → *not a data centre*
> **AI content generation** → hyperscale GPU → *yes — and it is new*

> Here is the fact that settles it, and it runs the opposite direction from
> the rumour:
>
> **Traffic coming from a data centre is the marker the ad industry uses to
> FLAG that traffic as non-human.** That is how fraud gets detected. That is
> why fraud operators *rent* capacity and why platforms publish data-centre IP
> lists to exclude it.
>
> **The campus in New Albany is where ad inventory gets made and where fraud
> gets filtered. It is not where the bots live.**
>
> Saying otherwise is association by adjacency — a big building near a thing
> you do not like. **That is the same move as the videos I am complaining
> about, and I am not going to make it.**
>
> **[BEAT]**
>
> But automated fake traffic sold as a real audience — that is real, and it
> has been prosecuted.

> **[CARD] UNITED STATES v. ZHUKOV · E.D.N.Y. · 1:18-cr-00633 · filed 2018-11-27**
> **[CARD] Eight defendants**

> An advertising botnet. Charged by the United States. Eight names on the
> indictment. **Docket number on screen — go read it.**
>
> **That is what the real thing looks like when it is proven. It looks like a
> docket, not a vibe.**

## DO NOT SAY — cut list for the edit

- ❌ **"Data centres are bot farms."** Refuted in Segment 4. If it survives the edit, the episode is worthless.
- ❌ **"Intel got CHIPS Act money through DARPA."** `HR00111830002` is DARPA's *Common Heterogeneous Integration* programme, signed 2017 — **five years before the Act.** Two things, one acronym.
- ❌ **Any framing of the Minnesota prosecutions as a fact about a community.** The indictment names defendants, amounts, programmes and dates. **It does not name a community, and the moment you do, you have left the record and handed away everything true you were carrying.**
- ❌ **Any characterisation of Federal Register documents `2025-19928`, `2026-00512` or `2026-00596`.** Titles only so far. **Unread is unsayable.**
- ❌ **"Ntrepid ran persona operations against Americans."** Not in this register. Not in any register I have.
- ❌ **Any total that sums across instrument families.** A contract, a grant and the face value of a loan guarantee are three different facts in the same units.
