# Conserved Currents

*An algorithmic philosophy for the Sentinel Report's ledger signature*

In a ledger nothing appears from nowhere and nothing vanishes without a trace. Conserved Currents takes that rule of bookkeeping as its physics. Every movement of money in the evidence becomes a current: a stream of particles released at the payer and drawn toward the payee through a field that belongs to this ledger alone. The field is built from the ledger's SHA-256 digest. Its sixty-four hexadecimal digits become the amplitudes, frequencies, orientations and phases of seven interfering harmonics. The result is a vector field with a single identity. Change one fact and the digest changes, the field changes, and every current bends differently. The image is a checksum you can see. The same file always yields the same picture, and anyone can rebuild it from the published data to confirm.

The currents obey the desk's own rules. Kinds of money never merge. Each kind keeps its own color and its own scale, and a stroke's weight is measured only against rows of the same kind, never against a grand total. A particle leaving the payer follows the field when it is far from its destination and yields to attraction as it closes in, so its path records both the ledger's global character and its own specific obligation. When the record does not say where money went, as with recipients unnamed in every filing reviewed, the particle has no destination. Its trail breaks into widening dots and fades. That is how the picture shows absence without inventing a destination.

Each stroke is drawn twice, as in double-entry bookkeeping. A bright trace carries the kind's color. A faint counter-trace runs beside it, offset along the normal, as the matching entry. Where currents arrive, their postings build up as fine arcs around the recipient, and the density of those rings shows how often the record names that party. Verified rows are drawn solid. Rows the machine could not check are drawn broken, so the image separates the proven from the reported the way the ledger does.

The system is seeded end to end, with no call to an unseeded random source anywhere. One pseudo-random generator is initialised from the digest's first 128 bits and drives the jitter of release points, the lifetimes of particles and the grain of the final surface. Timing is fixed as well. The animation runs a set number of integration steps, and a reader who prefers reduced motion gets the identical final frame computed at once. The animated version and the still one must match pixel for pixel.

The work has to look like it took countless hours to tune, because that is the standard the evidence behind it meets. The harmonic weights sit where the field produces broad, legible rivers instead of noise. Attraction ramps smoothly so currents arrive with intent and never snap. Alpha builds slowly enough that dense corridors glow and sparse ones stay visible. The composition leaves quiet space around the busiest hubs. It should read as a master-level implementation, refined parameter by parameter, where every number is justified. Within that restraint the currents should still surprise: rivers braid, eddies form where unrelated flows cross, and the whole picture settles into an order that could only come from this set of records.
